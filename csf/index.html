Retired standard

Certified Significant Form has been retired

The Musical Form Institute has consolidated its certification work around the Certificate of Embodied Production. Certified Significant Form is no longer an active program, and new submissions are not being accepted.

Certificate of Embodied Production View the public registry

Recordings certified under CSF remain in the public registry, and those certifications stand. The published CSF standards remain available below for reference.

For certification going forward, see the Certificate of Embodied Production.

Archived standards

The Certified Significant Form public standard, retained for reference. No further versions will be issued.
