Certification governance

Certification Governance

The CEP Public Standard defines what a recording must satisfy to be certified. The CEP Certification Governance Framework explains how the Musical Form Institute administers and controls that certification: analyst independence, conflicts of interest, evidence review, appeals, suspension and revocation, data handling, and control of the certification mark.

The CEP Public Standard

Defines what a recording must satisfy: the production conditions it must meet and the criteria an analyst evaluates against the published standard.

The Governance Framework

Defines how the Institute administers the certification: how decisions are made and kept independent, how evidence and appeals are handled, and how the mark is controlled.

Current framework

CEP Certification Governance Framework

Version 2.2

Applies to the Certificate of Embodied Production, currently the Institute’s only active certification. It sets out how certifications are decided and kept independent of production interests, how evidence is reviewed, the grounds for suspension and revocation, how submitted data is handled, and how the CEP certification mark is controlled.
