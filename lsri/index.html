The Local Sound Recording InitiativeA program of the Musical Form Institute

Recording Ecosystem Lab

A facilitated planning process for local music workforce and infrastructure

Bring the people who make local recordings into one room. Pay them for their knowledge. Leave with a plan the city can act on.

Discuss a Lab for Your City

What it is

During the Lab, stakeholders map the people, skills, spaces, institutions, and investment that support local recording, then establish shared priorities for action.

Following the workshop, the LSRI produces a Local Recording Ecosystem Action Plan with prioritized, costed options for implementation.
