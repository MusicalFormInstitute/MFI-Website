Certification standards

The Certificate of Embodied Production is the Institute’s only active certification, issued under a published and versioned standard. Certified Significant Form has been retired; its final standards remain archived below.

Process certification

Certificate of Embodied Production

CEP Public Standard v1.4 · August 5, 2026

Certifies a recording was produced without grid correction, pitch correction, or generative AI used to create, supply, replace, or reconstruct musical or lyrical content. Each submission is individually reviewed by an analyst against the published standard. Master audio is deleted after review; only a SHA-256 hash is retained on the public record.

Retired standards

These certifications are no longer offered. Existing certifications remain valid and searchable in the public registry, and the final published standards are retained for reference.

Retired

Certified Significant Form

Final version v2.2 · July 21, 2026

Certified that a recording was produced without generative AI. The Institute has consolidated its certification work around the Certificate of Embodied Production. Recordings certified under CSF remain in the public registry.
